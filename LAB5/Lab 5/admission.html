Admission Requirements for Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT)

To be eligible for admission to UMT, applicants must meet the following requirements: Academic Qualifications: Applicants must have completed their secondary education with a recognized qualification such as Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM) or its equivalent. Minimum Grades: Applicants should have achieved a minimum grade point average (GPA) as specified by the university for their chosen program of study. Language Proficiency: Proficiency in the English language is essential. Applicants may be required to provide proof of English language proficiency through standardized tests such as IELTS or TOEFL. Additional Requirements: Some programs may have specific prerequisites or additional requirements, such as interviews or portfolios. Applicants should check the specific requirements for their desired program.
