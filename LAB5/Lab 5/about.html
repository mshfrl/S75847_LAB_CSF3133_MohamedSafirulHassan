History of Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT)

Emblem of Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia. (2001–2007) Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, located at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu. It provided facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and also researches.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the Centre transformed into a branch campus, being renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. The name of the faculty was also changed to the Faculty of Applied Sciences and Technology. Also formed were the Faculty of Science and Professional Literature and the Matriculation Centre.

Later on, the Cabinet of Malaysia had approved the establishment of Terengganu University College (KUT) on 5 May 1999 as an associate campus of UPM.[7] Then Terengganu Universiti College was given autonomy on 1 May 2001 and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) on 20 June 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was given the status of a full-fledged university, and with that elevation, it was renamed again and remain to this very day as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. At the same time, KUSTEM's emblem was rehashed as the University's emblem. In 2015, the university is granted autonomy status. Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) began with a Fisheries and Ocean Sciences Centre in 1979, which provided training facilities for students in the Fisheries and Ocean Sciences Programme, in addition to providing research facilities for lecturers.

Through the academic programme restructuring at UPM (Universiti Pertanian Malaysia), the entire Faculty of Fisheries and Ocean Sciences was transferred to Terengganu and given a new name: the Faculty of Applied Sciences and Technology (FSGT) starting in June 1996. The Faculty of Professional Sciences and Arts (FSSI) and the Matriculation Study Centre (PPM) were also established.

From June 1996, this campus was internally recognised (by UPM) as a centre of responsibility and named Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu Branch (UPMT), headed by a Rector (designate). The Cabinet, in its meeting on May 5, 1999, agreed to approve the proposal for the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT), based on the Fisheries and Ocean Sciences Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu.

The Kolej Universiti Terengganu (Incorporation) Order 1999 (PUA 292) was approved by the House of Representatives on July 26, 1999. KUT was an associate campus of UPM, and its students were awarded degrees from UPM. KUT was granted autonomous power as Kolej Universiti Terengganu on May 1, 2001. On July 1, 2001, KUT officially changed its name to Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia, abbreviated as KUSTEM.

Starting on February 1, 2007, corresponding to 13 Muharam 1428 H, another chapter in the Malaysian education system was created. In an effort to strengthen the position of the country's public higher education institutions (IPTAs), six University Colleges underwent a rebranding. Today, KUSTEM is known as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.
